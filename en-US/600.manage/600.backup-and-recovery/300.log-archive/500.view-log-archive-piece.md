# View piece information

After log archiving is enabled, you can query for information about pieces from views.

## Background information

This topic describes how to view pieces in log archiving. For more information about pieces, see [Overview of log archiving](../300.log-archive/100.overview-of-log-archive.md).

## View all pieces of all tenants from the sys tenant

You can query the `CDB_OB_ARCHIVELOG_PIECE_FILES` view in the `sys` tenant for all pieces of all tenants in the current cluster.

1. Log in to the `sys` tenant of the cluster as the `root` user.

   In the preceding example, the tenant with the tenant ID `1002` has four pieces.

   For the four pieces of tenant `1002`:

   * `round_id` is `1` for the three pieces with the piece IDs `1` to `3`. This means that the three pieces are generated in the first round of archiving. The piece with the piece ID `4` is generated in the second round of archiving. Logs whose SCNs fall within the range from `'2022-06-01 06:00:00.000000'` to `'2022-06-02 05:59:00.000000'` are archived to piece 1. The amount of raw log data is 25 GB, and the amount of archived data is 25 GB, with a compression ratio of 1.00. The value of the `FILE_STATUS` column of piece 1 is `DELETED`. This indicates that the data in this piece has expired and been deleted. If the value of the `STATUS` column of a piece is `FROZEN`, the data in this piece is complete and no more logs will be archived to this piece.

   * The value of the `FILE_STATUS` column is `AVAILABLE` for the three pieces with the piece IDs `2` to `4`. This indicates that the data in the three pieces is not deleted and can be accessed. For piece 3, `CHECKPOINT_SCN` changes to `FROZEN` before `'2022-06-04 06:00:00.000000'`. This is because log archiving is disabled and no more logs will be archived to piece 3. Currently, tenant 1002 is undergoing the second round of archiving, and piece 4 is the first piece generated during this round of archiving. The status of piece 4 is `ACTIVE`, indicating that logs are being archived to the piece.

## View all pieces of a user tenant

You can query the `oceanbase.DBA_OB_ARCHIVELOG_PIECE_FILES` view from a user tenant for all pieces of the tenant.

1. Log in to the database as the tenant administrator of the user tenant.

   <main id="notice" type='explain'>
   <h4>Note</h4>
   <p>The administrator user of a user tenant is the <code>root</code> user.</p>
   </main>

2. View all pieces of the current tenant.
