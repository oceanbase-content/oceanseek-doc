# Overview of OBKV-HBase management

This topic describes the O&M and management operations in OBKV-HBase.

## Cluster management

The tenant in OBKV-HBase is a user tenant. The O&M and management operations on an OBKV-HBase cluster are the same as the general cluster O&M and management operations in OceanSeek.

For more information, see [Cluster management](../../../../600.manage/100.cluster-management/100.cluster-management-overview.md).

## Tenant management

The tenant in OBKV-HBase is a user tenant. The O&M and management operations on an OBKV-HBase tenant are the same as the general tenant O&M and management operations in OceanSeek.

For more information, see [Tenant management](../../../../600.manage/200.tenant-management/100.tenant-introduction.md).

## Replica management

The replica O&M and management operations in OBKV-HBase are the same as the general replica O&M and management operations in OceanSeek.

For more information, see [Replica management](../../../../600.manage/300.replica-management/100.replica-introduction.md).

## High availability

The high availability of OBKV-HBase relies on the high availability of the entire link, namely, the high availability of OBServer nodes, the proxy, and the client on the link. OBKV-HBase inherits the high availability capability of OceanSeek on the server side and supports both direct connection mode and proxy mode in deployment.

OceanSeek provides a variety of technologies for ensuring service high availability, including intra-cluster multi-replica disaster recovery, arbitration-based disaster recovery, and inter-cluster physical standby database disaster recovery. For more information, see [High availability in OceanSeek](../../../../600.manage/400.high-availability/100.disaster-recovery-management-overview.md).

For more information about the high availability of the OBKV-HBase link, see [High availability of OBKV-HBase](50.obkv-hbase-high-availability.md).

## Security and permissions

As the ultimate data carrier, OceanSeek prioritizes data security. It provides a comprehensive set of enterprise-level security features, including identity authentication, access control, and data encryption, which effectively ensure the security of user data.

For more information about security and permissions in OBKV-HBase, see [Security and permissions in OBKV-HBase](100.obkv-hbase-security.md).

## Backup and restore

OBKV-HBase reuses the backup and restore capability of OceanSeek.

For more information, see [Backup and restore](../../../../600.manage/600.backup-and-recovery/100.overview-of-physical-backup-and-recovery.md).
