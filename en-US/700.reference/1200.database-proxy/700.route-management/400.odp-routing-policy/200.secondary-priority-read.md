# Follower-first read

You can specify the user-level session variable `proxy_route_policy` to make ODP first read data from a follower. This setting takes effect only in weak consistency read mode. When the setting is in effect, data is first read from a follower instead of a replica selected by the load balancer.

Log in to the business tenant from a supported client tool. Execute the following statement to set the `proxy_route_policy` variable:

```sql
obclient> SET @proxy_route_policy='[policy]';
```

Valid values of the `[policy]` parameter are as follows:

1. `follower_first`: ODP reads data first from a follower, even if the cluster is undergoing major compaction. The priority order is as follows:

   Follower not undergoing major compaction in the same IDC --\> Follower not undergoing major compaction in a different IDC in the same city --\> Follower undergoing major compaction in the same IDC --\> Follower undergoing major compaction in a different IDC in the same city --\> Leader not undergoing major compaction in the same IDC --\> Leader not undergoing major compaction in a different IDC in the same city --\> Follower not undergoing major compaction in a different city --\> Follower undergoing major compaction in a different city --\> Leader not undergoing major compaction in a different city --\> Leader undergoing major compaction in a different city

2. `unmerge_follower_first`: ODP reads data first from a follower that is not undergoing major compaction. The priority order is as follows:

   Follower not undergoing major compaction in the same IDC --\> Follower not undergoing major compaction in a different IDC in the same city --\> Leader not undergoing major compaction in the same IDC --\> Leader not undergoing major compaction in a different IDC in the same city --\> Follower undergoing major compaction in the same IDC --\> Follower undergoing major compaction in a different IDC in the same city --\> Follower not undergoing major compaction in a different city --\> Leader not undergoing major compaction in a different city --\> Follower undergoing major compaction in a different city --\> Leader undergoing major compaction in a different city

3. Other values: The normal load-balanced routing logic in weak consistency read mode is adopted. The priority order is as follows:

   Leader or follower not undergoing major compaction in the same IDC --\> Leader or follower not undergoing major compaction in a different IDC in the same city --\> Leader or follower undergoing major compaction in the same IDC --\> Leader or follower undergoing major compaction in a different IDC in the same city --\> Leader or follower not undergoing major compaction in a different city --\> Leader or follower undergoing major compaction in a different city
