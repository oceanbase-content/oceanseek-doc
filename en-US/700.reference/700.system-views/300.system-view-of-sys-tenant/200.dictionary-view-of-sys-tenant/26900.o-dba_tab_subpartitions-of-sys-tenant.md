# oceanbase.DBA_TAB_SUBPARTITIONS

## Overview

Displays the subpartition information of the partitioned tables for the current tenant, including the subpartition name, the name of the table and its partition, their storage attributes, and the statistics generated by the `DBMS_STATS` package.

## Sample query

