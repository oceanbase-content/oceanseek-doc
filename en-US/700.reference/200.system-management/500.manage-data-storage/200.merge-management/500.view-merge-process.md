# View major compaction information

After initiating a major compaction, you can view the major compaction information through views.

The duration of a major compaction depends on the volume of data changed between two major compactions. The larger the data change, the longer the major compaction takes. In addition, the number of compaction threads and the cluster load during the major compaction also affect the duration.

## View major compaction information of all tenants from the sys tenant

The sys tenant can view the tenant-level and partition-level major compaction information of all tenants by querying views.

1. Log in to the `sys` tenant of the cluster as the `root` user.

2. Execute the following statements to view the major compaction information:

    * Query the `CDB_OB_ZONE_MAJOR_COMPACTION` or `CDB_OB_MAJOR_COMPACTION` view for tenant-level major compaction information.

      The `CDB_OB_ZONE_MAJOR_COMPACTION` view displays the major compaction progress of all zones in all tenants.

      ```shell
      obclient> SELECT * FROM oceanbase.CDB_OB_ZONE_MAJOR_COMPACTION;
      ```

## View major compaction information of the current tenant

A user tenant can view the major compaction information of the current tenant by using a view.

1. Log in to the database as the tenant administrator of the user tenant.

2. Execute the following statements to view the major compaction information.

    * Query the `DBA_OB_ZONE_MAJOR_COMPACTION` or `DBA_OB_MAJOR_COMPACTION` view for the tenant-level major compaction information of the current tenant.

      ```sql
      obclient [oceanbase]> SELECT * FROM oceanbase.DBA_OB_ZONE_MAJOR_COMPACTION;
      ```

      * `MEDIUM_MERGE`: Partition-level major compaction.
      * `MINI_MERGE`: Mini compaction, converting the MemTable into a Mini SSTable.
      * `MINOR_MERGE`: Minor compaction, combining multiple Mini SSTables into a new Mini SSTable, or combining multiple Mini SSTables with one Minor SSTable into a new Minor SSTable.
      * `META_MAJOR_MERGE`: A special type of compaction that combines data from before a specified point in time into a Meta Major SSTable. Its data format is the same as that of a Major SSTable, excluding multi-version data and uncommitted transaction data.

      For more information about the other fields in the `GV$OB_TABLET_COMPACTION_HISTORY` view, see [GV$OB_TABLET_COMPACTION_HISTORY](../../../700.system-views/400.system-view-of-mysql-mode/300.performance-view-of-mysql-mode/4200.gv-ob_tablet_compaction_history-of-mysql-mode.md).
