# OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP)

OceanBase Cloud Platform (OCP) was developed alongside OceanBase AI Database. It is an enterprise-level database management platform with OceanBase at its core.

OCP provides full-lifecycle management of components such as OceanBase AI Database clusters and tenants, and also manages OceanBase-related resources such as hosts, networks, and software packages. It enables you to manage OceanBase AI Database clusters more efficiently and reduce the IT O&M costs of your enterprise.

As an enterprise-level database management platform for OceanBase, OCP has a wealth of product features:

* **Efficient OceanBase AI Database cluster management**

  * Supports high availability.

  * Responds to requests in seconds.

* **Clear information framework**

  * The new object-oriented architecture provides an immersive management perspective for individual clusters and tenants, better fitting user habits.

  * Functional modules in the immersive navigation are organized based on DBA work scenarios, making feature access clearer.

* **Smooth and efficient user operations**

  * Core jobs are designed with chained tasks, delivering a clear and uninterrupted process.

  * OCP provides quick access to O&M tasks, allowing you to switch to these tasks from any page.

* **Visual experience**

  * The task details page provides flowcharts and a natural, flexible canvas interaction, making task progress management more intuitive and efficient.

  * Newly added topology diagrams of clusters and tenants reveal distributed business logic and display running status and key data.

* **Professional features**

  * Provides full-lifecycle management of OceanBase AI Database clusters.

  * Management functions designed based on OceanBase AI Database characteristics.

  * Progressive management from clusters to sessions.

  * Carefully designed topology features.
